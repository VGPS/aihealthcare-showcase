## Executive summary

This specification transforms content generation for the AI in Healthcare application from ad-hoc guesswork into an evidence-driven, source-verified editorial pipeline.

Every item in this calendar connects directly to:
1. Current legal and regulatory requirements (effective 2025–2026 or with 2026–2027 enforcement milestones).
2. Documented clinical adoption and implementation bottlenecks (such as ambient documentation scaling and prior-authorization timelines).
3. Active consumer financial rules and fraud trends (such as 2026 IRS contribution limits, student loan repayment shifts, CFPB rule stays, and AI-driven investment schemes).
4. Durable evergreen financial literacy questions (credit utilization, tax-advantaged account hierarchies, and healthcare cost management).

## Search demand validation methodology

To maintain institutional-grade integrity and protect the application from hallucinated search figures, search demand is validated across two distinct channels:

1. **Qualitative demand verification (Completed):** 20 targeted search-query clusters were executed across web and regulatory indexes. Active search intent was confirmed through regulator announcements, court proceedings, legislative committee analyses, and peer-reviewed clinical studies.
2. **Search-volume governance policy (Engineered):** 
   - Automated exploration of public trend interfaces during research encountered automated rate limiting (HTTP 429), and direct SEO tool APIs were not connected in this session.
   - **Policy:** Never fabricate monthly search volume numbers.
   - Instead, every calendar item in the machine-readable dataset includes a `search_validation` record defining `query_candidates`, validation status, required validation gates before publication, and priority tier assignments (`P0`, `P1`, `P2`).
   - Time-sensitive regulatory and fraud-warning topics (`P0`) are governed by event timing and legal impact rather than search volume alone, while evergreen topics (`P2`) require first-party Search Console or keyword validation before large production investments.

## Effort calibration model

To optimize team velocity and resource allocation, each piece is assigned an explicit effort tier:

- **S (Short): 2–4 hours.** Single authoritative source, tightly focused explainer or tactical checklist (800–1,200 words).
- **M (Medium): 4–8 hours.** Multi-source analysis, comparison table, workflow map, or decision tree (1,200–2,000 words).
- **L (Large): 8–14 hours.** Deep-dive framework, original operational template, economic model, or implementation canvas (2,000+ words).
