Drafted 2026-10-21. Use this guide when you return.

## How to Access Sales Navigator

1. Log into LinkedIn normally at linkedin.com
2. Look at the top navigation bar — there is a grid/waffle icon (9 dots) in the upper right
3. Click it → select **Sales Navigator** from the dropdown
4. Or go directly: **linkedin.com/sales/home**
5. Your trial dashboard appears — this is Sales Navigator Core

---

## The Interface (first time)

When you land in Sales Navigator you will see:
- **Home** — your feed and alerts
- **Search** (top bar) — for finding people and companies
- **Lists** — where you save Lead Lists (your organized prospect groups)
- **Alerts** — notifies you when saved leads change jobs, get promoted, post content

The key tool for this task is **Lead Search** (click Search → search for People).

---

## How to Search "Connections of" an Analyst

This finds 2nd-degree connections via a specific person — the analysts you already contacted.
Do this once per analyst. Takes about 3 minutes each.

### Step-by-step:

1. Go to **Search → Lead filters** (the filter panel opens on the left)
2. Scroll down to **"Connections of"** filter (it's in the Relationship section)
3. Type the analyst's name → select them from the dropdown
4. Add these additional filters to narrow results:
   - **Job Title** → type: `Analyst` (will suggest options — pick "Analyst" as keyword)
   - Add a second title: `Research Director`
   - Add a third: `Research Manager`
   - **Industry** → select: Hospital & Health Care, Health, Wellness and Fitness,
     Information Technology and Services (pick all three)
5. Click **Search**
6. Review results — look for people at health IT research firms, consulting firms,
   advisory firms, or large health systems
7. Click **Save to List** (top right of results) → create a new list called
   e.g. "Analyst Network — [Analyst Name]"

## After Building the Lists

Once you have 1–3 Lead Lists built:

1. Go to **Lists → Lead Lists**
2. Open a list → review each person
3. For people who look like a fit (Analyst / Research Director titles at relevant orgs):
   - Click their name → view their profile
   - If "Connect" is available → use it with a personalized note (≤200 chars)
   - If only "Message" (Open Profile) → use the analyst message template from AnalystOutreach.md
   - If InMail → use one of your 50 monthly credits
