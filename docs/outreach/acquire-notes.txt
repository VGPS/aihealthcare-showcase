Q: CAN THE FEATURES JUSTIFY $100K?
============================================================

SHORT ANSWER: No, not by themselves.

WHAT DOES JUSTIFY $100K:
  1. The historical data archive is unreplicable.
     Continuously harvested since early 2025, never deleted. A buyer cannot
     recreate a time machine — they can only buy it from you. This is your
     strongest argument. Lead with this, not the feature list.

  2. Replacement cost is genuinely high.
     3,000+ tests, hexagonal architecture, 14+ feature slices, multi-LLM
     integration, Stripe billing, SAML2 SSO, async enterprise data-export
     system. A contractor team would bill well into six figures to rebuild.
     This is your second-strongest argument.

WHAT HURTS YOUR CASE:
  - Zero revenue. Marketplace buyers price pre-revenue assets on cash flow,
    not engineering effort. Expect most offers on Acquire.com/Flippa to land
    below $100K without a strategic buyer.
  - Feature breadth can backfire. 14 verticals with no usage signal reads as
    "scope crept without product-market fit." Cut the feature list in your
    listing to 2-3 genuinely hard-to-replicate things.

HOW TO IMPROVE THE ODDS:
  - Offer 20-40 hours of paid transition support at closing. Buyers discount
    pre-revenue technical assets heavily when the builder might disappear.
    Continuity de-risks the deal for them.
  - Target strategic buyers (see outreach list), not generic marketplace buyers.
    A healthcare intelligence firm or compliance-tech shop has a monetization
    path and will pay more than a flipper.
