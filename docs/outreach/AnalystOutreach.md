## Strategy Overview

The right frame is NOT "we are a vendor seeking coverage."
The right frame is: "We have primary-source data you can cite — a continuous, structured record
of every FDA clearance, CMS rule, state law, funding round, and market signal in AI healthcare."

Analysts are data-hungry. They write annual Hype Cycles and predictions reports by hand.
A platform that produces this data continuously is a primary source they want to know about.

**Lead with an insight, not a pitch.** Before requesting a briefing, share one specific
data point that would be useful to that analyst's coverage area. Examples:
- "We've tracked 83 FDA clearances for AI devices in 2026 — here's the trend by category"
- "8 state AI-health laws from 7 states take effect January 1, 2027 — the convergence only
  shows up when watching all 58 simultaneously"

**Recommended first contact:** Mutaz Shegewi at IDC (#6) — the most publicly accessible
of the group. He's active on LinkedIn AND Twitter (@TazShegewi) and responds to direct outreach.
Use him to test messaging before approaching Gartner analysts (longer lead times).

### Gartner
- **Non-client path:** Vendor Briefing form at gartner.com/en/contact/vendor-briefings
- **Do NOT** cold-email Gartner analysts for briefing requests — it routes wrong
- Lead time: 4–8 weeks to actual call (can stretch to 3 months for in-demand analysts)
- Format: 45 min, no sales pitch, strictly informational
- LinkedIn outreach to build relationship BEFORE formal briefing submission is fine

### Forrester
- **Non-client path:** forrester.com/help/analyst-briefing/
- Lead time: 3–4 weeks (faster than Gartner)
- Shannon Germain Farraher's email is publicly listed: sgermain@forrester.com

### IDC Health Insights
- **Non-client path:** idc.com/about/contactidc OR direct LinkedIn (IDC analysts more accessible)
- Mutaz Shegewi is active on LinkedIn and X — direct connection is fine
- IDC also sells "sponsored research" (white papers) — separate paid track, not needed here
- Analysts are particularly data-hungry; cite specific numbers in first contact
