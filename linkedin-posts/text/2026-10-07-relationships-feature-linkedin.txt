PitchBook charges $30,000–$60,000/year to track M&A deals and company relationships.

CB Insights starts at $15,000/year for healthcare tech intelligence.

A Bloomberg Terminal — if you want relationship data with market context — runs $25,000/year.

We built continuous, AI-extracted company relationship tracking into Big Sky Labs AI Healthcare for under $50/month.

Here's how it works.

Every day our pipeline scans 30 days of AI healthcare articles through a three-stage process:

**Stage 1 — Keyword filter.** 29 relationship phrases (partnerships, acquisitions, investments, integrations) identify candidate articles. Fast and cheap. No data leaves the system yet.

**Stage 2 — LLM classification.** Candidate articles go to an AI model that reads the full context, identifies the two companies, classifies the relationship type, and produces a confidence score and natural-language summary. If it can't find two clearly named company parties, it returns NO_RELATIONSHIP and the article is excluded. This is the quality gate.

**Stage 3 — Directory canonicalization.** Extracted company names are normalized against a 627-company AI healthcare directory to resolve spelling variants — "R1 RCM" becomes "R1," "Microsoft Corporation" becomes "Microsoft." Health systems and universities that appear as partners are left as-is; they're valid participants, just not in the vendor directory.

The result: a continuously updated relationship graph showing WHO is partnering with whom, WHO is acquiring whom, and WHERE investment is flowing — specific to the AI healthcare space, sourced from live news, with evidence articles you can trace back to.

PitchBook covers thousands of industries with editorial staff entering data 2–7 days after the fact. We cover one sector automatically, with same-day extraction from the same news your analysts are reading.

Different tools for different jobs. But if AI healthcare deal flow is your job — you probably don't need the $30K option.
