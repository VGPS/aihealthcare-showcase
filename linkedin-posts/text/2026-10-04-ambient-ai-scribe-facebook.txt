=== POST BODY ===

Ambient AI companies claim their tools save physicians 2 hours per day on documentation.

That sounds like a solved problem.

It isn't.

---

Here's what the independent data actually shows:

→ JAMA multisite study (2025, five academic medical centers): 16 minutes saved per 8-hour day on average. Heavy users hit 27 minutes. Not 2 hours.

→ The only randomized controlled trial (NEJM AI, 238 physicians, 14 specialties): 1.7% less time-in-note — not statistically significant.

→ Atrium Health post-implementation study (NEJM AI, 2024): no significant improvement in key efficiency metrics at the system level.

→ The one consistent finding across all studies: burnout decreases. JAMA Network Open found burnout dropped from 51.9% to 38.8% after 30 days. That's real — and arguably the most important outcome.

---

The time savings are real. The 2-hour benchmark is not.

Vendor claims come from self-reported physician time studies. Physicians overestimate how long they spend on tasks they find unpleasant — documentation being the canonical example. Controlled measurement produces much smaller numbers.

---

In fee-for-service medicine: faster notes → more patients → more revenue. The ROI math closes.

In employed physician models: the health system captures the gain. The physician's documentation burden drops. Their RVU targets stay the same. Their compensation doesn't change.

Adoption resistance often isn't about the AI. It's about who benefits from the saved time.

---

In value-based care: volume doesn't drive revenue. The time savings only generates ROI if it reduces burnout, expands panel size, or improves quality scores — none of which appear in a 90-day vendor pilot.

The market is pricing every ambient AI deployment as if it's fee-for-service.

---

No one in the pilot is asking the right question: What does the saved time actually become?

More patients. Earlier departure. Less burnout. Or just different paperwork.

The vendors don't fund that study. Most health systems haven't built the measurement infrastructure to find out.
