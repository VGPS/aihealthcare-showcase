## What this means for vendors

The obvious reaction is "Big Tech has always circled healthcare." True — but circling and tying for the top of a live relevance ranking are different things, and the difference shows up in how deals actually get won.

A hospital system evaluating a point-solution vendor is very often *already* an Azure or AWS enterprise customer. Microsoft doesn't need to win a new procurement cycle to sell that hospital an ambient-documentation module — it needs to expand a contract that already exists. That's a structurally cheaper sales motion than anything a standalone vendor can run, independent of whose model performs better.

The competitive question for healthcare AI startups just changed. It's no longer "how do we compare to the last three point-solution vendors this buyer evaluated." It's "why would this buyer choose us over expanding a platform contract they already have."
