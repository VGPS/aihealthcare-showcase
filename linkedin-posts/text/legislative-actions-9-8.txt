What's notable isn't the volume — it's the convergence.

92% require human review of AI decisions. 75% mandate disclosure when AI is used. 58% explicitly ban autonomous AI claim denials.

9 states enacted all three — independently. No model legislation. No coordinated effort. Alabama, California, Colorado, Georgia, Iowa, Nebraska, Texas, Utah, and Washington arrived at the same answer on their own.

These aren't conflicting approaches. They're converging on the same three requirements: human review required, disclosure mandatory, no autonomous denial decisions.

If you're building AI for healthcare insurance, clinical workflows, or patient-facing chatbots — your compliance clock is already running. 10 of these laws take effect between now and January 2027.
