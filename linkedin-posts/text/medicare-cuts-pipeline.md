# Medicare Cuts + AI: 6-Week Content Pipeline
### Topic: How AI Will Operationalize $536B in Medicare Cuts

**Core thesis:** Congress doesn't cut Medicare spending by sending letters. It cuts spending by
authorizing denial. At $76B/year by 2034, the only operational mechanism that can process that
volume is AI automation. The companies doing it are already in federal court. The tools exist.
The incentive just got enormous.

**Voice:** Bill's personal voice — 72, Medicare-eligible, no deference to corporate America.
Direct, sourced, unapologetic.

---

## The Numbers (always cite these)

- **$536B** over 10 years (AMA / House Budget Committee Democrats)
- **~$45B** immediate Medicare cut triggered by PAYGO sequestration (Medicare Rights Center)
- **$76B/year** by 2034 unless Congress intervenes (Medicare Rights Center)
- Trigger: Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act, activated by deficit increase in recent budget
  reconciliation legislation
