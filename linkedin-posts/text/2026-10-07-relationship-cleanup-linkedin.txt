"2025" acquired "Alliance." "Re" invested in "Expanded Ambulatory Access."

Those aren't companies. They're bugs. And they were live on my platform.

Here's what happened:

→ My Company Relationship Graph extracts "X acquires Y" / "X partners with Y" from healthcare AI news using keyword matching — grab the words around a trigger phrase, build a graph edge. No real entity recognition, just a word window.
→ It worked, until I actually read the Source column. Years. Generic nouns. Sentence fragments standing in as company names.
→ I had two options: write a denylist and chase garbage forever, or fix the actual problem.

I rebuilt extraction using the same pattern the app already uses for deal-signal detection — Claude classifies the keyword-matched candidate articles directly, extracting real source/target names instead of a fixed word window.

Then a second pass: resolve every extracted name against the 627-company directory the app already maintains, so "R1" and "R1 RCM" collapse into one canonical entity instead of two.

Ran a retroactive cleanup against the live data: 116 relationships → 58. Half the table was duplicate noise — including a case-sensitivity bug in the dedup check that had been quietly letting near-identical rows through for weeks, unrelated to the original fix, found while cleaning up.

Real company names. Real summaries. A duplicate problem fixed at the root instead of patched with more rules.
