5 notable legal/regulatory developments this week, spanning active litigation, regulatory action and compliance requirements.

COMPETITOR WATCH
5 competitor updates tracked across OpenAI, Amazon, and Epic Systems.

1. ⚠️ **[LEGAL] ChatGPT's Medical Advice Nearly Killed a Florida Man — Lawsuit Against OpenAI Could Rewrite AI Liability Law**
First major test case for holding AI companies liable when patients follow chatbot medical advice.

2. ⚠️ **[LEGAL] FDA Clears First Software as Medical Device With Patient-Facing LLM**
Landmark clearance opens a regulatory pathway for generative AI that directly interacts with patients.

3. ⚠️ **[LEGAL] Less Than 1% of FDA-Cleared AI Devices Tested for Clinical Benefits**
Of 1,357+ cleared devices, fewer than 1% have evidence of improving patient outcomes.

4. ⚠️ **[LEGAL] Healthcare Providers Lack Guidance on Legal Liability When AI Goes Wrong**
No clear framework exists for who is liable — the developer, the hospital, or the physician.

5. ⚠️ **[LEGAL] CT Announces AI Regulations for State Employee Health Plans**
Connecticut becomes latest state to regulate AI in healthcare insurance decisions.

6. ✅ **[COMPETITOR] OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Health With Epic Integration and Medical Records Access**
ChatGPT now connects to EHRs, health apps, and public health data sources.

7. ✅ **[COMPETITOR] Amazon Connect Health Brings Agentic AI to Point of Care**
AWS launches autonomous AI framework for clinical decision support at the bedside.

8. ✅ **[COMPETITOR] Epic Previews New Interoperability Features for Providers and Developers**
Epic expanding its developer ecosystem for AI integration.

9. ✅ **[COMPETITOR] ARPA-H Launches $63M Effort to Build FDA-Authorized AI Agents for Heart Failure Care**
Federal investment in autonomous clinical AI agents — a new category.
