## Legal & Regulatory

### 1. ChatGPT's Medical Advice Nearly Killed a Florida Man — Lawsuit Could Rewrite AI Liability Law

A Florida pastor followed ChatGPT's health advice and nearly died. The resulting lawsuit against OpenAI is the first major test case for holding AI companies liable when patients follow chatbot medical advice. The outcome could establish precedent for hundreds of similar cases.

*Source: CBS News* | *Topic: AI Healthcare Legal*

### 2. FDA Clears First Software as Medical Device With Patient-Facing LLM

The FDA has cleared the first software-as-a-medical-device that includes a patient-facing large language model. This landmark clearance opens a regulatory pathway for generative AI that directly interacts with patients — setting precedent for hundreds of pending applications.

*Source: McGuireWoods* | *Topic: AI Healthcare Legal*

### 3. Less Than 1% of FDA-Cleared AI Devices Tested for Clinical Benefits

Of more than 1,357 AI/ML devices cleared by the FDA, fewer than 1% have been tested for patient-centered outcomes like mortality and morbidity. The 510(k) pathway requires only "substantial equivalence" — not clinical proof.

*Source: TechTarget* | *Topic: AI Healthcare Legal*

### 4. Healthcare Providers Lack Guidance on Legal Liability When AI Goes Wrong

No clear legal framework exists for determining liability when AI-assisted clinical decisions cause harm. Is the developer liable? The hospital? The physician who relied on the recommendation? Courts are being asked to answer questions that regulators haven't addressed.

*Source: Economist Enterprise* | *Topic: AI Healthcare Legal*

### 5. Connecticut Announces AI Regulations for State Employee Health Plans

Connecticut becomes the latest state to regulate AI in healthcare insurance decisions, joining a growing patchwork of state-level AI governance that healthcare organizations must navigate.

*Source: CT Mirror* | *Topic: AI Healthcare Legal*

## Competitor Watch

### 1. OpenAI Launches ChatGPT Health With Epic Integration and Medical Records Access

OpenAI launched ChatGPT Health, connecting the platform to electronic health records via Epic, health apps, and public health data sources. Healthcare organizations can now surface EHR data directly within ChatGPT conversations.

*Source: Fierce Healthcare* | *Topic: OpenAI Healthcare*

### 2. Amazon Connect Health Brings Agentic AI to Point of Care

AWS launched an agentic AI framework for healthcare — autonomous clinical decision support that operates at the bedside, moving beyond back-office automation into real-time care delivery.

*Source: AWS* | *Topic: Amazon Connect Health*

### 3. Epic Previews New Interoperability Features for Providers and Developers

Epic is expanding its developer ecosystem with new interoperability features, signaling a more open approach to third-party AI integration within its dominant EHR platform.

*Source: Fierce Healthcare* | *Topic: Epic Systems Healthcare AI*

### 4. ARPA-H Launches $63M Effort to Build FDA-Authorized AI Agents for Heart Failure Care

The federal government committed $63 million to develop autonomous AI agents specifically authorized by the FDA for heart failure care — a new category of clinical AI that operates independently within defined clinical guardrails.

*Source: Fierce Healthcare* | *Topic: AI Healthcare Legal*

### 5. OpenAI Launches GPT-6 Astra Model

OpenAI's next-generation model includes healthcare-specific capabilities, continuing the company's aggressive push into clinical applications following the ChatGPT Health launch.

*Source: Healthcare IT News* | *Topic: OpenAI Healthcare*
