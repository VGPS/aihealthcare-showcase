This week's clearest signal isn't a funding round or a product launch — it's a chart.

**State AI-healthcare legislation just went vertical.**

I maintain a public registry of every enacted state AI-healthcare law — 40 laws across 26
states since the first ones appeared in 2024. Here's the year-over-year count:

  2024 → 3 laws
  2025 → 11 laws
  2026 → 27 laws (through October, with two months still on the clock)

2026 alone has produced nearly twice as many new laws as 2024 and 2025 combined. Nineteen
states passed something this year — Colorado led with four separate bills, Rhode Island
passed three. There's no federal mandate driving this. These are independent legislatures,
in different states, converging on the same handful of concerns: consumer chatbot disclosure,
mental-health AI guardrails, and limits on AI-driven payer utilization review.

If you're building, selling into, or investing in healthcare AI and "state law" is still a
someday-item on your roadmap, this is the week to move it up. The pace isn't slowing down —
it's compounding.
