Everyone has an opinion on which healthcare-AI news "matters." I stopped guessing and started measuring it directly: for every market-moving headline, I capture the stock price at the moment of the news and again at 1 hour, 4 hours, 1 day, and 3 days out, across every category — earnings, regulatory, funding, M&A, partnerships.

The pattern that surprised me most: regulatory news moves price slower but more durably than earnings news. An FDA clearance often shows a muted 1-hour reaction but a meaningfully larger 3-day move once the market finishes digesting what it actually means for the product pipeline. Earnings beats do the opposite — sharp 1-hour pop, frequently fading by day 3 once the initial reaction gets re-priced against guidance detail.

If you're building a thesis around "this category of news is the leading indicator," multi-horizon price tracking is the cheapest way to actually test that instead of assuming it.
