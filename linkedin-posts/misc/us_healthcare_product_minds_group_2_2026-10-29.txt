Every healthcare product team keeps some version of an ecosystem map — who's partnered with whom, who acquired whom, who's investing in whom. Almost everyone I've talked to maintains it by hand, which means it's always a little behind and always someone's unglamorous side project.

Mine updates itself. An AI pipeline reads daily healthcare news and extracts partnership, acquisition, and investment relationships between companies automatically, with every extracted relationship traceable back to the specific article it came from — no silent "trust the graph" step. New edges appear the same day the news does, not whenever someone remembers to update a slide.

The traceability matters more than the automation, honestly. An auto-generated graph nobody can audit is just a fancier way to be confidently wrong. One where every connection links back to its source article is something you can actually defend in a roadmap review.

For the product folks here: is ecosystem/partnership mapping something your team tracks formally, or does it live mostly in people's heads until someone needs it for a specific deck?
