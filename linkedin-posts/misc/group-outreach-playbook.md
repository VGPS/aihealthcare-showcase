# LinkedIn Group Outreach Playbook — BigSkyLabs / AI in Healthcare

Generated: September 10, 2026

## Strategy

28 groups is too many to work simultaneously. Focus on Tier 1 (direct AI healthcare)
first — that's where ideal connections live. The mega-groups (1M+ members) have low
ROI for intros because posts get buried in volume.

**Cadence:**
- Week 1 (Sep 10-14): Introduce yourself in 4 Tier 1 groups (one per day, Mon-Thu)
- Week 2 (Sep 15-19): Introduce yourself in 4 more Tier 1 groups
- Week 3+: Share infographics into introduced groups + introduce in Tier 2
- Ongoing: 5 min/day commenting in active groups

**Rules:**
- Never post the same text in two groups on the same day
- Wait 3+ days between intro and first share in the same group
- Every post ends with a question to invite replies
- No external links in intro posts — earn credibility first
