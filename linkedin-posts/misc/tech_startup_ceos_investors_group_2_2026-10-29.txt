Comparable-deal analysis in healthcare M&A is usually manual: read the press release, dig for the 8-K if it's public, guess at the split between upfront cash and milestone payments if it's private. I automated the first pass of that.

An LLM reads every deal headline and summary my platform harvests and pulls out structured terms where they're disclosed: upfront cash, milestone payments, equity stake percentage, royalty percentage, and — critically — flags which portion of the deal was actually disclosed versus which portion is being estimated or omitted. Most deal announcements disclose less than people assume; knowing exactly what wasn't disclosed is as useful as knowing what was.

This doesn't replace reading the actual filing before you commit capital. What it does is let you screen 50 deals in the time it used to take to read 5, so you know which ones are worth the deep read.
