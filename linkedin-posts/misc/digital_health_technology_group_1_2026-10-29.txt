A 600+ company directory is useless if it's just an alphabetical list. The question anyone evaluating a vendor or partner actually has is: is this company gaining momentum right now, or just loud?

I built a signal score instead of a static listing: article velocity over 90 days, whether they've closed a funding round recently, and a sentiment penalty if recent coverage has turned negative. Companies get flagged 🔥 trending, 💰 recently funded, or ⚠ on the watch list based on actual signal, not self-reported marketing copy. You can sort the whole directory by relevance, trending, funded, or watchlist instead of scrolling an unordered list hoping to recognize a name.

The part that mattered most in practice: the ⚠ watchlist flag (declining sentiment + stalled activity) turned out to be just as useful as the 🔥 trending flag. Knowing who's cooling off is as actionable as knowing who's heating up, especially before a partnership or procurement decision.

For the digital health folks here: when you're vetting a vendor or potential partner, what's your actual signal for "this company has real momentum" versus "this company has a good pitch deck"?
