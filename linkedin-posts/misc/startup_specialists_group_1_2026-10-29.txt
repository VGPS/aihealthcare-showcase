Nobody warns you about this before you ship an AI feature: every user action can cost you money, and if you don't meter it, your margin disappears one API call at a time.

My app calls 6 different LLM providers depending on the feature — search synthesis, sentiment scoring, deal classification, social post generation. Early on I let every tier use all of them freely. The bill told me that was a mistake within a month.

What fixed it wasn't cutting features — it was tier-gating usage at the port level: free users get a capped number of AI-backed actions per day, paid tiers get more, and the heaviest pipelines (multi-model synthesis) are gated harder than the cheap ones (single-model classification). The product didn't get worse. The economics just stopped being invisible.

The lesson for anyone building an AI feature into a product: price the AI call before you price the subscription. If you don't know your cost-per-active-user on the AI line item, you don't actually know if you have a business yet — you have a demo with a credit card attached.

What's your approach been to metering AI costs without it feeling punitive to users?
