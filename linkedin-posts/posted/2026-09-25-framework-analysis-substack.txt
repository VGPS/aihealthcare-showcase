# Scoring the Big Four in Healthcare AI

There is no shortage of takes on who is "winning" in healthcare AI. AWS has the enterprise
infrastructure. Anthropic has the safety narrative. OpenAI has the consumer mind share.
Google has the data relationships with health systems. These are all reasonable observations.
They are also impossible to compare in any systematic way — until you break them apart by
dimension.

That is what the Framework Competitive Analysis feature on BigSkyLabs.ai does.

---

## The six dimensions

Competitive position in healthcare AI is not one thing. A company can have a genuinely
mature technical product and still be thin on clinical validation. A platform with strong
regulatory positioning can be weak on developer experience. The composite score — "Company X
is an 8/10" — flattens all of that into something that sounds authoritative but tells you
nothing about the shape of the advantage.

The six dimensions scored are:

**Technical Maturity** — How developed is the underlying AI product for healthcare-specific
use cases? Does the coverage reflect real deployment depth or mostly early-stage capability
claims?

**Clinical Validation** — Is there evidence of peer-reviewed studies, real-world clinical
outcomes, or health system partnerships that speak to effectiveness in actual care settings?

**Regulatory Positioning** — How proactively has the company engaged with FDA, CMS, and
ONC frameworks? Is there a track record of cleared products, or is the regulatory story
still largely prospective?

**Platform Strategy** — Is the company building a standalone product or positioning as
infrastructure that other builders run on? Is there evidence of an ecosystem, or is it
still a point solution?

**Market Momentum** — What does recent coverage velocity look like? Is the company gaining
ground in news frequency, deal announcements, and analyst attention? Or has the chatter
plateaued?

**Developer Experience** — For the companies offering API access and build-on-top pathways,
how does the coverage characterize the integration experience? Are developers choosing this
platform by preference or by default?

---

## How the scores are derived

Each dimension score (1–10) is generated by analyzing recent article coverage through
a structured LLM prompt that extracts dimension-specific signal. The analysis is grounded
in actual harvested articles — not a one-time survey, not a vendor self-report. When the
platform harvests new coverage, the score can be refreshed.

Every score links back to the source articles that drove it. You can read the dimension
rationale and then click through to the underlying coverage.

The radar chart renders all four companies overlaid — AWS HealthLake, Anthropic Claude
for Healthcare, OpenAI health products, and Google Health AI — so the competitive
shape of each is immediately visible without having to hold four separate profiles
in your head.

---

## What the visualization reveals

The radar chart is more useful than a ranked list because it shows the shape, not just
the position. A company that is strong across five dimensions and genuinely weak on one
is a different competitive situation than a company that is moderate across all six.
The first has a gap to exploit or a risk to watch. The second is a different kind of
competitor entirely.

The detail page behind each company shows:

- The per-dimension score and bar chart
- The LLM-generated rationale for each score
- A bullet-point breakdown of strengths and weaknesses
- Recent developments pulled from coverage
- The full list of source articles that fed the analysis
