A competitor announces an acquisition. Now you need: is the sentiment around them trending up or down lately? How do they score on your competitive framework? Any open regulatory items that might slow the deal down? What does their company profile actually say? That's normally 4 separate tabs and 20 minutes of synthesis before you can write the one-paragraph "here's what this means for us" Slack message leadership actually wants.

I collapsed that into one view. Every deal signal in my platform auto cross-references against sentiment history, framework score, regulatory events, and company profile for both parties, the moment the deal is detected — so the synthesis step that used to take 20 minutes of tab-switching is just... already done when you open the page.

It's a small thing architecturally (join 4 data sources instead of 1), but it's the difference between "I'll get back to you on what this means" and answering in the same meeting the news broke.

For the PMs here: when a competitor move happens, how long does it typically take your team to go from "we saw the headline" to "we have an actual take"?
