Here's the part that actually matters.

Most AI healthcare market news arrives pre-spun — a "partnership" that's still a pending LOI, a funding number that's a target commitment rather than a closed round, a "launch" that's really a regional pilot. None of that makes the reporting dishonest. It just means a CONFIRMED deal and a SPECULATIVE rumor can show up in the same sentence without anyone flagging the difference.

We built the thing that flags the difference.

The Market Digest runs daily at noon UTC. It researches AI healthcare market news with Perplexity Sonar, classifies each item with Claude (CONFIRMED vs. SPECULATIVE), ranks it by market impact (1 = highest), and tags every public company with its ticker symbol and peer group before storing the result as a permanent dated record.
