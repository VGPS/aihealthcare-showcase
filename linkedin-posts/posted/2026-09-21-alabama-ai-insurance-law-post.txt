In 11 days, Alabama becomes the latest state to ban insurers from using AI as the sole basis to deny your health care claim.

SB 63 takes effect October 1, 2026. Here's what it does:

- Insurers cannot use AI alone to deny, reduce, or defer health care services
- Any adverse coverage determination based on medical necessity must be made by a licensed physician
- Insurers must disclose their use of AI in utilization review

Alabama is not alone. 17 states now have laws restricting AI in insurance prior authorization and utilization review:

AL, AZ, CA, CO, FL, GA, IA, IN, MD, MN, NE, TX, UT, WA — plus federal CMS rules.

27 new AI healthcare laws were enacted in 2026 alone. Eight more take effect January 1, 2027.

The pattern is clear: states are not waiting for Congress. They are building the regulatory framework themselves — one bill at a time, across 26 states and counting.

The question for health plans and health tech companies: are your AI systems compliant with the laws that just took effect in your states?
