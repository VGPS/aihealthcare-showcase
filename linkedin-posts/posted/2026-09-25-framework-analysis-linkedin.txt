Most "competitive analysis" in healthcare AI is someone's opinion dressed up in a slide deck.

I wanted something grounded in actual coverage.

So I built a system that:

→ Tracks every article mentioning AWS HealthLake, Anthropic Claude for Healthcare,
   OpenAI health products, and Google Health AI
→ Feeds them into a structured LLM analysis across 6 dimensions
→ Scores each company 1–10 on Technical Maturity, Clinical Validation, Regulatory
   Positioning, Platform Strategy, Market Momentum, and Developer Experience
→ Renders it as a radar chart so the shape of each competitor's strengths
   (and gaps) is visible at a glance

The radar chart is the part I keep coming back to. When you see all four overlaid,
the differentiation is immediate — not which company has the biggest marketing budget,
but where each one actually has depth vs. where the coverage is thin.

Every score links to the source articles that drove it.

Two screenshots below — the multi-company overview and a single-company detail breakdown.
