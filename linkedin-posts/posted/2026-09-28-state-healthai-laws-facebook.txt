The categories cover:
• Payer utilization review — AI-driven coverage denials now have legally required human review thresholds in multiple states
• Claims downcoding by AI — some states specifically prohibit AI from reducing reimbursement amounts during automated processing

• Patient consent — required before AI is used in patient-facing diagnosis or treatment recommendations

• Comprehensive AI acts — broader framework laws that treat healthcare as a high-risk domain
