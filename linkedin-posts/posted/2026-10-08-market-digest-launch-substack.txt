## Why market news needs a fact-check layer

AI healthcare news moves fast, and a lot of it arrives pre-spun: a "launch" that's really a pilot, a "partnership" that's a pending LOI, a funding number that's a target commitment rather than a closed round. None of that makes the reporting dishonest — it just means a reader scanning headlines can't always tell confirmed fact from speculative framing without digging into the source.

We built a pipeline that does that digging automatically, every day, for one sector: AI in healthcare.

---

## What the Market Digest does

The Market Digest at `/dashboard/market` runs daily at noon UTC. It:

1. **Researches** AI healthcare market news via Perplexity — regulatory actions, funding rounds, M&A, product launches, partnerships.
2. **Classifies** each item with Claude: category (REGULATORY, FUNDING, M_AND_A, PRODUCT_LAUNCH, MAJOR_PARTNERSHIP, LEGAL_ACTION, WORKFORCE), fact classification (CONFIRMED vs. SPECULATIVE), and a market impact rank from 1 (highest) to 5 (lowest).
3. **Enriches** any mentioned public company with live market data — ticker symbol, peer group — via Alpaca.
4. **Tracks** regulatory actions longitudinally by jurisdiction and rulemaking stage, instead of reporting them once and moving on.
5. **Persists** the result as a permanent, dated digest — not a feed that scrolls away.

The CONFIRMED/SPECULATIVE split is the part that matters most. A verified FDA 510(k) clearance and an unconfirmed funding rumor are never presented the same way. If the pipeline can't verify a claim independently, it says so.

## What we've tracked so far

As of today:

- **199 market-moving entries** logged since the digest went live
- **118 confirmed, 81 speculative** — roughly 59% of everything tracked has independent verification; the rest is labeled as reported-but-unconfirmed, not silently upgraded to fact
- **Last 30 days by category:** 51 regulatory actions, 45 funding events, 40 product launches, 36 major partnerships, 13 M&A deals, plus legal and workforce items
- **84 private funding rounds tracked, $16.07 billion combined** — the largest: a $600 million round for Angle Health, and $500 million commitments each for Ortet and Thoreau
- Public companies are tagged by ticker and peer group when they're affected — Tempus AI (TEM), R1 RCM (RCM), Novo Nordisk (NVO), Eli Lilly (LLY), Twist Bioscience (TWST) all appear in the tracked set

## What it isn't (yet)

In the interest of the same honesty the digest itself is built around: the pipeline has architecture in place for multi-horizon price-reaction scoring (how a stock moves 1h/4h/1d/3d after news breaks) and analyst rating-change tracking, but neither has live data populated yet. Those are the next two pieces to turn on. What's live today is the fact-classification, impact ranking, regulatory tracking, and funding-round tracking described above.

We also noticed, pulling these numbers, that a couple of funding rounds appear to be double-logged (the same company and amount recorded twice from two separate source mentions). That's a dedup gap on the list to fix — similar to one we found and fixed in the company relationship tracker last week. The underlying classification is accurate; the row-level dedup needs tightening.
