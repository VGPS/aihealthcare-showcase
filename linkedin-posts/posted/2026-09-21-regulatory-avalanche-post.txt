While Congress debates AI safety frameworks, 26 states have already passed 58 laws regulating AI in healthcare.

Not proposed. Enacted. With enforcement teeth.

Here's what the data shows:

17 laws restrict AI in insurance prior authorization — your health plan can no longer use an algorithm alone to deny coverage in Alabama, California, Colorado, Florida, Georgia, Indiana, Iowa, Maryland, Minnesota, Nebraska, Texas, Utah, Washington, and more.

19 laws restrict AI in mental health and psychotherapy — California, Colorado, Hawaii, Idaho, Illinois, Iowa, Maine, Nebraska, Nevada, New York, Oregon, Rhode Island, Utah, Vermont, and Washington have drawn a hard line at AI therapists.

16 laws regulate AI companion chatbots — from California's SB 243 to New York's Safe by Design Act.

9 comprehensive AI governance acts are now on the books — Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, plus three federal executive orders.

27 of these 58 laws were enacted in 2026. Eight more take effect January 1, 2027.

This is not a future problem. If you build, sell, or deploy AI in healthcare, the regulatory map already has sharp edges — and most compliance teams haven't mapped them yet.
