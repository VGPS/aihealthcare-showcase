"How would you describe your startup?"

AI in Healthcare (bigskylabs.ai) is a Spring Boot SaaS that turns scattered
healthcare-AI news, FDA/CMS regulatory filings, state legislation, and company
signals into one sourced, LLM-classified intelligence feed — built so a market
research analyst never has to manually track 100+ sources by hand.

Every output traces back to a dated source article, and news is explicitly
labeled CONFIRMED vs. SPECULATIVE. A public AI Liability Incident Tracker logs
AI-healthcare lawsuits, FDA flags, and regulatory sanctions, with an LLM
auto-classifier and a human review queue behind it — a level of depth and
provenance that general healthcare-news aggregators like Definitive Healthcare,
CB Insights, and Fierce Healthcare don't go to. The platform even exposes an
agent-ready MCP server, so external AI tools can query the data directly.

Under the hood: Java 21, Spring Boot, Spring AI across four LLM providers
(Claude, GPT, Perplexity, Gemini), Postgres + pgvector, 3,200+ automated tests,
and 15+ self-operating pipelines running 24/7 on AWS with no daily human
intervention required.

I built this solo, motivated by watching healthcare workers struggle to
understand what AI tools actually do and where they fail. The product,
infrastructure, and data moat are done — three-plus years of compounding
article history, regulatory tracking, and wiki provenance that a new entrant
can't shortcut. What's missing is distribution: the business is pre-revenue
not because the product doesn't work, but because I haven't had the bandwidth
to run both the engineering and the sales motion. That's the opportunity for
the next owner.
