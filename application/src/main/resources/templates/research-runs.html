View the audit trail of every research pipeline run — both manually triggered queries and daily automated harvests. Each record shows the query, retrieval mode, citation count, and timestamp so you can track research activity over time.

How It Works
  1. Every research query — whether from the Compare, AI Search, or Vendor Compare pages — is recorded as a run.
  2. The daily Research Harvest Scheduler also creates runs automatically for each configured topic.
  3. Click any run to view the full research answer, citations, and synthesis details.
