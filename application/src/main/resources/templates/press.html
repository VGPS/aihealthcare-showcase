What Claude Code actually did

Roughly 80% of this codebase was written by Claude Code. I directed the architecture, reviewed diffs, and made judgment calls on tradeoffs. Claude handled the repetitive scaffolding, adapter implementations, test generation, prompt engineering, and most of the Thymeleaf UI. The Spring AI integration and multi-LLM verification pattern were particularly well-handled — I described what I wanted and it produced production-quality code on the first pass most of the time.

What the platform does

1

Harvests and deduplicates articles from PubMed, openFDA, the Federal Register, Healthcare Dive, Beckers, Google News, Y Combinator, and 50+ other sources daily.

2

Runs an 11-step pipeline — sentiment scoring, competitive framework analysis, deal signal detection, regulatory matching, trend detection, wiki compilation, and newsletter generation.

3

Synthesizes intelligence through Claude (Anthropic) across 7 domains simultaneously — regulatory, deals, sentiment, frameworks, trends, articles, and wiki.

4

Cross-verifies with a second AI — GPT-4o, Gemini 2.5 Flash, or AWS Bedrock Nova independently evaluate every claim, assign confidence scores, and cite source references.
