LinkedIn Daily Post

Manual

Generates a ready-to-copy LinkedIn post from today's top-weighted articles (last 24 hours, capped at 5). Produces two blocks: a post body without links (avoids LinkedIn reach penalty) and a source links block for the first comment.

Posting workflow

  1. Click Open Generator to get today's ready-to-copy text.
  2. Copy the Post Body and paste into a new LinkedIn post. Publish it.
  3. Immediately open the published post → Add a comment → paste the Source Links block.
  4. Submit the comment. LinkedIn pins your own first comment automatically.

Why links in a comment? LinkedIn's algorithm suppresses posts with external URLs by 40–60%. Keeping links in the comment preserves reach while still providing sources.

Page: /dashboard/linkedin
Est. duration: Instant (reads from database)
Best time to post: Tue–Thu, 7–9 AM local time (highest LinkedIn B2B engagement)

Facebook Daily Post

Manual

Generates a ready-to-copy Facebook post from today's top-weighted articles (last 24 hours, capped at 5). Same article pipeline as LinkedIn. Post body ≤ 390 chars — lead story above the "See more" fold, additional headlines as bullets. Links are OK in Facebook posts (no reach penalty).

Posting workflow

  1. Click Open Generator to get today's ready-to-copy text.
  2. Copy the Post Body and paste into a new Facebook Page post. Publish it.
  3. Open the published post → Write a comment → paste the Comment block for full sources.
  4. Submit the comment.

Facebook vs LinkedIn: Links in the post body do NOT hurt Facebook reach. The site URL is included directly in the post.

Page: /dashboard/facebook
Est. duration: Instant (reads from database)
Best time to post: Weekdays, 9–11 AM local time
