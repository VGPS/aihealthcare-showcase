Setting up SSO

  1. Obtain your IdP's Entity ID, SSO URL, and signing certificate from your IT team.
  2. Click "Add Provider" and enter the IdP details. The Registration ID becomes the URL slug.
  3. Share your SP metadata URL with the customer's IT team: /saml2/service-provider-metadata/{registrationId}
  4. Once the IdP is configured on both sides, the "Sign in with..." button appears on the login page.
