Subscriber Feature

The Social Post Generator creates ready-to-copy LinkedIn and Facebook posts from your daily market digest — with platform-specific formatting, character limits, and posting instructions built in.

Upgrade to Subscriber

How It Works

Pulls the top 5 ranked entries from the latest market digest and formats them for each platform. LinkedIn: no links in post body (40-60% reach suppression), source links go in the first comment, 2,900 char limit. Facebook: links OK in body (no penalty), lead headline above the 390-char "See more" fold, bullets for additional stories. Copy each section with one click and follow the platform-specific posting steps below.

No market digest available to generate posts from.

Run the Market Digest pipeline first from the Pipelines page.
