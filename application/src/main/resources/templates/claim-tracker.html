Frontier AI Claim Tracker

Evidence quality ratings for AI company public claims

Source: Claims are automatically extracted from articles harvested by the RSS, Perplexity, and research pipelines — the same articles powering the newsletter. The pipeline runs daily and on-demand from the Admin Pipelines page.

How claims are identified: An LLM reviews each article and extracts specific factual claims made by frontier AI companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Microsoft, NVIDIA, etc.) about their own products or capabilities. It ignores analyst opinions and focuses only on first-party statements.

Verdict definitions:

  • Evidence-Backed — claim cites peer-reviewed research or independently verified benchmarks
  • Alleged/Unverified — claim stated by the company without a verifiable citation (most common — tech announcements rarely include citations)
  • Marketing Hype — superlative language ("best", "most advanced", "revolutionary") with no supporting data
  • Contradicted — claim conflicts with other sourced evidence in the knowledge base
  • Retracted — the company subsequently walked the claim back

Why mostly Alleged/Unverified? This is expected. News articles quote company press releases and blog posts, which rarely include direct citations. EVIDENCE_BACKED requires third-party verification in the article text itself — uncommon in general tech reporting. As the wiki grows with contradicting evidence, CONTRADICTED counts will rise.

How to use: Click any sortable column header to sort. Use the filter bar to narrow by company, verdict, or claim type. Admins can generate LinkedIn + Facebook post drafts from any claim. SUBSCRIBER+ can export all claims as CSV.
