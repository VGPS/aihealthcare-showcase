Substack Posting Steps

    2. 
                    
  2. Set the title: "AI in Healthcare — Weekly Intel Roundup"
    3. 
                    
  3. Set the subtitle: the date range shown above
    4. 
                    
  4. Copy the Article Body above
    5. 
                    
  5. Paste into the Substack editor (it renders Markdown automatically)
    6. 
                    
  6. Add a cover image (screenshot of this page or a graphic)
    7. 
                    
  7. Under Settings:
                        
      
                            
    • Section: choose or create "Weekly Roundup"
      • 
                            
    • Check "Send to email subscribers"
      • 
                            
    • Tags: AI Healthcare, Legal, Competitors, Weekly Roundup
      • 
                        
    
                    
    8.

Substack Quick-Start Tips

                
    
                    
  • Free vs. paid: start with all posts free to build audience; gate premium content later
    • 
                    
  • Sections: create a "Weekly Roundup" section so subscribers can filter
    • 
                    
  • Schedule: Saturday 9:00 AM CT is ideal for weekend reading
    • 
                    
  • Cross-post: after publishing, share the Substack link on LinkedIn as a separate post during the week
    • 
                    
  • Notes: use Substack Notes (their short-form feed) to tease individual stories from the roundup during the week
    • 
                    
  • Subtitle: acts as the email preview text — make it count
    • 
                    
  • Tags: help Substack's discovery algorithm surface your content
    •
