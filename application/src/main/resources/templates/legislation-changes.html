How Source Monitoring Works

Every Monday at 10:30 UTC, the Legislation Source Monitor re-fetches each law's source URLs and computes a SHA-256 hash of the page content. When the hash differs from the last known value, a change event is recorded here. Changes indicate the law text may have been amended, the agency may have updated their guidance, or the source URL may have moved or gone offline. Review each change and click the affected law to check for updates.
