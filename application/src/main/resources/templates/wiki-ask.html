Purpose

Prompt any healthcare AI question and get an answer grounded in the wiki's compiled knowledge base. Unlike a web search, answers draw on curated, source-tracked wiki pages — providing deeper longitudinal context that goes beyond individual articles.

How It Works
  1. Type a natural-language question and optionally select which AI models to use.
  2. The system searches the wiki for pages most relevant to your question.
  3. Each selected AI model synthesizes an answer from the matched wiki content, citing specific pages as evidence.
  4. Answers include links to the source wiki pages so you can verify claims and explore further.
