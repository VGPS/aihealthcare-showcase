Dimension definitions:

1. Technical Maturity — Evaluate the readiness of healthcare-facing APIs, SDKs, and
   integration tooling. Consider: Is there a documented healthcare API surface? Are
   there published SDKs for clinical system integration (HL7 FHIR, DICOM, CDA)?
   How mature is the developer documentation for healthcare use cases? What integration
   patterns exist for EHR platforms (Epic, Cerner/Oracle Health, MEDITECH)?

2. Clinical Validation — Assess the depth of clinical evidence supporting the platform.
   Consider: Are there peer-reviewed studies in clinical journals? Has the technology
   been evaluated in prospective clinical trials? Is there published real-world evidence
   from health system deployments? What clinical endpoints have been measured?

3. Regulatory Positioning — Evaluate compliance infrastructure and regulatory clearances.
   Consider: Does the company offer a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement? Are there
   FDA 510(k) clearances, De Novo authorizations, or PMA approvals for any products?
   What is the SOC 2 / HITRUST posture? Is there evidence of engagement with ONC or
   CMS certification programs? How does the company handle PHI in model training and
   inference?

4. Platform Strategy — Analyze the ecosystem and business model for healthcare.
   Consider: What is the partnership landscape (health systems, EHR vendors, pharma,
   payers)? Is there a healthcare-specific pricing model or tier? How significant is
   platform lock-in risk for health system buyers? Does the company offer on-premises
   or VPC deployment for data sovereignty requirements?

5. Market Momentum — Evaluate commercial traction and competitive trajectory.
   Consider: What recent deals, partnerships, or customer wins are documented? Is press
   coverage accelerating, stable, or declining? How does the company's healthcare
   narrative compare to six months ago? Are there signals of expanding or contracting
   healthcare investment?

6. Developer Experience — Assess the practical experience of building healthcare
   applications on the platform. Consider: What is the quality of healthcare-specific
   SDK documentation and code samples? Is there a developer community focused on
   healthcare use cases? How easy is it to integrate with clinical data standards?
   Are there healthcare starter templates, reference architectures, or sandbox
   environments with synthetic clinical data?
